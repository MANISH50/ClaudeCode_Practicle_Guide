About StayVista

A demo hotel-booking experience built with Python, Flask, SQLite and Bootstrap.

Our story

Travel planning without the clutter

StayVista shows how a clean, friendly booking journey can work end to end: search, filter, compare, save, book and cancel, all with instant feedback.

Everything you do is saved to a private SQLite database that exists only for your browser session, so you can explore freely without affecting anyone else.
